Bike parking at Guelph's plazas

Some of Guelph's plazas make it easy to arrive by bike, and they deserve credit for it. 31 of 68 open-air plazas have earned stars so far, going by the racks in OpenStreetMap as of 2026-11-03.

A plaza without stars has not failed anything. OpenStreetMap is a volunteer map, so there may be a rack nobody has recorded yet, and if there really is none, that is a chance for a first rack. Found one? Open the plaza's link, click Edit, and add a point tagged amenity=bicycle_parking.

A plaza here is open-air, multi-tenant retail with shared surface parking: named plazas and shopping centres, plus unnamed retail areas with at least four named businesses mapped inside. Big-box power centres count. Enclosed malls, single-tenant buildings and downtown storefronts do not. Ratings use only what the map records, so a missing rack type or capacity can hold a plaza back, and each card says what earns its next star. "Rack next door" means a rack within 100 m of the property but not on it. The full report has every exclusion and the data as CSV.
